## How a CRT TV works

<div class="scene wide" data-scene="crt" data-viewbox="0 0 1600 760"></div>

Notes:
A cathode ray tube is a gun that fires electrons at glass painted with phosphor. Wherever the
beam lands, it glows. Magnets steer the beam: the dot means field out of the screen, which bends
it up; flip the current, the cross means field into the screen, and it bends down. The bigger
the symbol, the stronger the field. Steer it fast enough, in a regular pattern, and
you have a picture.
