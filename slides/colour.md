<p class="note">(Wiggle not to scale. The real one is 4.43 MHz: 230 cycles a line.)</p>

Notes:
How do you add colour without breaking every black and white set? Keep the brightness signal
exactly as it was, and sneak a fast wiggle on top: a 4.43 megahertz subcarrier. A black and white
set barely notices. A colour set reads the size of the wiggle as saturation, and its timing, its
phase, as hue. Timing relative to what? Just after every sync there are ten cycles of reference:
the colour burst. "This is what zero degrees looks like."
